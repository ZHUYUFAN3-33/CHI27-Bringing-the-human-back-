Study 2 (Track A) — precision and power
========================================================================

1. Worst-case precision of one proportion (Wilson 95%, p = .50)
   The estimand: P(human involved), P(AI only), P(can't tell), per clip.

     usable n    half-width
          200          6.9 pp
          250          6.2 pp
          270          5.9 pp
          300          5.6 pp
          330          5.4 pp
          385          5.0 pp
          400          4.9 pp

2. Precision away from .50, at n = 300 (the recommended target)

    true share    half-width
          20%          4.5 pp
          30%          5.2 pp
          40%          5.5 pp
          50%          5.6 pp
          60%          5.5 pp
          70%          5.2 pp
          80%          4.5 pp

3. The 'majority' rule: Wilson 95% lower bound above 50%
   Power at a given true share, and the n that reaches 80%.

    true share   power @300   n for 80%
          55%         43%         777
          60%         94%         198
          65%        100%          89
          70%        100%          48
          75%        100%          29

   A true share of .55 needs a sample this study will not have, so a
   'majority' claim is only in reach if the real split is lopsided.
   Report the interval either way; the majority rule decides wording,
   not whether there is a result.

4. The conditional disability denominator
   DIS is interpretable only among people who inferred a person is
   involved, and that share is itself an outcome. At 300 usable:

    infer a human   denominator   half-width @ p=.50
             30%            90                10.1 pp
             40%           120                 8.8 pp
             50%           150                 7.9 pp
             60%           180                 7.2 pp
             75%           225                 6.5 pp
             90%           270                 5.9 pp

   This is why the primary disability reporting is the full WHO x DIS
   table over everyone, with the conditional estimate as the second
   step rather than the only one.

5. Within-person clip differences (exact McNemar, simulated power)
   Same people answer all three clips, so the comparison is paired.
   rho is how strongly one person's two answers move together.

       n   clip A   clip B    rho    power
     200      50%      60%    0.0     46%
     200      50%      60%    0.3     60%
     200      50%      60%    0.6     76%
     200      50%      65%    0.0     83%
     200      50%      65%    0.3     92%
     200      50%      65%    0.6     98%
     300      50%      60%    0.0     65%
     300      50%      60%    0.3     78%
     300      50%      60%    0.6     92%
     300      50%      65%    0.0     95%
     300      50%      65%    0.3     98%
     300      50%      65%    0.6    100%

   At n = 300 a ten-point paired difference reaches 80% power only
   once the two answers are correlated (78% at rho = .3, 92% at .6);
   under independence it is 65%. A fifteen-point difference is
   comfortable throughout. So clip differences are powered for a
   sizeable gap, not for a small one, and the plan treats them as a
   secondary question reported with intervals either way.

========================================================================
Recommendation: 300 usable participants, recruit about 330 completers
to absorb 8-10% loss to the two prespecified quality checks. At 300
usable, no proportion is reported less precisely than +/-5.6 points.
