# Phase 1 Reference Verification Audit

Audit date: 2026-09-05. Scope: the 35 records included in `references.bib`.

## Rule and result

For each record, title, authors, year, venue and DOI were matched to a publisher/DOI record or, when automated access to the publisher was blocked, to an authoritative scholarly index, institutional record or author-hosted copy. Volume, issue, pages and article number were checked when applicable. This audit establishes bibliographic identity; it is not a systematic-review risk-of-bias assessment and does not independently reproduce every empirical claim in the annotations.

Result: **35/35 core records matched; 35 unique BibTeX keys; 35 unique DOIs; 0 unresolved records; 0 duplicate DOI records.**

The ACM Digital Library blocked direct automated retrieval during this pass. ACM entries were therefore triangulated through ACM DOI registration plus author/institutional copies carrying the ACM reference-format line, or DBLP/CiNii/institutional metadata. `DOI record` below links to the persistent resolver; the `Cross-check` column records the independent route used.
